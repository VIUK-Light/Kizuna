# Code of Conduct

Kizuna welcomes participation from people who contribute in good faith.
This policy applies to Issues, Pull Requests, code review, Discussions, and
other project spaces operated for Kizuna.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Respect user agency, privacy, safety, and different backgrounds.
- Assume good intent while addressing the impact of harmful behavior.
- Keep technical criticism focused on the work, evidence, and project goals.
- Protect private information and do not publish secrets or personal data.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or targeted personal attacks.
- Sexualized, hateful, or demeaning conduct toward participants.
- Publishing private information or conversations without permission.
- Deliberately disrupting project spaces or misrepresenting another person.
- Retaliating against someone who raises a good-faith concern.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not use a public Issue to report a sensitive conduct concern. Contact the
Kizuna maintainers through the repository's private communication channel and
include the affected project space, a factual description, and any safe
supporting context. Do not include secrets or personal data that are not
necessary to review the concern.

Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, request a correction,
or take other proportionate action. Reports are handled as confidentially as
the available project infrastructure permits. Security vulnerabilities must
follow SECURITY.md, not this process.

If the available private reporting channel is not visible, ask for the
maintainer contact path without posting sensitive details publicly. The
maintainer team should keep a private channel available before inviting a
large external contributor community.

## Scope

This policy complements CONTRIBUTING.md and its English version,
CONTRIBUTING.en.md. It does not replace GitHub's own terms or applicable law.

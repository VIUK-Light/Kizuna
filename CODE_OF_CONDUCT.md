# Code of Conduct

Kizuna welcomes participation from people who contribute in good faith. This
policy applies to Issues, Pull Requests, code review, Discussions, comments,
and other project spaces operated for Kizuna.

## 期待する行動

- Respect user agency, privacy, safety, and different backgrounds.
- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Assume good intent while addressing the impact of harmful behavior.
- Keep technical criticism focused on the work, evidence, and project goals.
- Protect private information and do not publish secrets or personal data.
- Follow the project's license, contribution rules, and GitHub's terms.

## 禁止する行動

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or targeted personal attacks.
- Sexualized, hateful, or demeaning conduct toward participants.
- Publishing private information or conversations without permission.
- Deliberately disrupting project spaces or misrepresenting another person.
- Retaliating against someone who raises a good-faith concern.
- Repeatedly contacting, mentioning, or pressuring a participant after they ask
  for the contact to stop.

## 非公開での報告

行動上の懸念を、公開Issue、Pull Request、Discussion、コメントへ詳細付きで
投稿しないでください。Kizunaでは、GitHubの **Reported content** を非公開の
一次報告経路として使用します。

1. 問題のあるIssue、Pull Request、Discussion、またはコメントを開きます。
2. 右上の `…` メニューから **Report content** を選びます。
3. 理由を選び、**Report to repository admins** を選択します。
4. 事実関係、対象のURL、緊急性、必要な対応だけを送信します。秘密情報や
   不要な個人情報は含めないでください。

この報告は公開コメントとして追加されず、リポジトリ管理者がGitHubの
**Settings → Access → Moderation options → Reported content** から確認します。
管理者は報告を確認し、必要に応じて内容の削除、会話のロック、参加制限、
警告、またはその他の比例した対応を行い、対応後に報告を解決済みとして記録します。

Reported contentが表示されない場合は、行動問題の詳細を公開しないでください。
GitHubのCommunity GuidelinesまたはTerms of Serviceへの違反、脅迫、個人情報の
公開などは、[GitHub Supportの非公開Report abuseフォーム](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse?category=report-abuse&report=other&report_type=unspecified)
へ報告してください。リポジトリ管理者自身が関係者の場合も、同じくGitHub Supportへ
報告し、管理者が自分の報告を処理しないようにしてください。

Maintainerは、Reported contentを常時有効にし、報告を受け取れる管理者を確認します。
この非公開報告経路が停止している場合、公開Issueを代替経路にせず、設定を復旧してから
プロジェクト固有の報告を受け付けます。

## 対応方針

リポジトリ管理者は、報告を可能な範囲で秘密として扱い、報告者の安全とプライバシーを
尊重します。対応内容や期限は、重大性、証拠、関係者の安全、GitHubの機能と規約に応じて
決定します。報告者への報復や、報告を理由にした不利益な扱いは認めません。

管理者自身が当事者である場合は、その管理者を対応から外します。管理者間で利益相反を
解消できない場合やGitHubの規約違反が疑われる場合は、GitHub Supportの判断と手続きへ
エスカレーションします。

## Private reporting

Do not describe a sensitive conduct concern in a public Issue, Pull Request,
Discussion, or comment. Kizuna uses GitHub **Reported content** as its primary
private reporting route:

1. Open the relevant Issue, Pull Request, Discussion, or comment.
2. Open the `…` menu and select **Report content**.
3. Choose the reason and select **Report to repository admins**.
4. Include only the facts, affected URL, urgency, and requested action needed
   for review. Do not include secrets or unnecessary personal data.

The report is not posted as a public comment. Repository administrators review
it under **Settings → Access → Moderation options → Reported content**, then may
remove content, lock a conversation, limit participation, issue a warning, or
take another proportionate action. They record the report as resolved after
the moderation work is complete.

If **Report to repository admins** is unavailable, do not publish the conduct
details. Use the [private GitHub Support Report abuse form](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse?category=report-abuse&report=other&report_type=unspecified)
for conduct that violates GitHub's Community Guidelines or Terms of Service,
including threats or exposure of private information. If a repository
administrator is involved, use GitHub Support instead of asking that person to
handle the report.

Maintainers must keep Reported content enabled and ensure that administrators
can review incoming reports. A disabled reporting route is an operational
failure, not a reason to require a public Issue; maintainers must restore the
private route before accepting project-specific conduct reports.

## Enforcement

Repository administrators are responsible for triage and proportionate
enforcement. Reports are handled as confidentially as GitHub's infrastructure
and the available evidence permit. Timelines and actions depend on severity,
the safety of participants, conflicts of interest, and applicable GitHub rules.
Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is not permitted.

If an administrator is involved, that person must not participate in the
decision. If the administrators cannot resolve a conflict of interest, or if
GitHub's rules may have been violated, the report should be escalated to GitHub
Support.
